# Leave and Working Hours Policy

Standard working hours at Acme Corp are 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday to Friday, with a one-hour lunch break. Hybrid staff work from the office on Tuesdays and Thursdays.

Full-time employees receive 15 vacation days and 10 sick days per year. Vacation requests should be submitted in the HR portal at least 5 working days ahead and need manager approval. Unused vacation days carry over up to a maximum of 5 days into the next year.

Sick leave of more than 2 consecutive days requires a medical certificate. Employees should notify their manager before the start of the workday when calling in sick.
