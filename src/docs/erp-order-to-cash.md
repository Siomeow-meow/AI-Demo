# ERP Lesson: Order-to-Cash

Order-to-cash (O2C) is the process from a customer's order to receiving their money.

Steps: 1) Quote or enquiry. 2) Sales order entered, with a credit check and availability check. 3) Pick, pack and ship from the warehouse, which reduces inventory. 4) Invoice the customer. 5) Customer pays. 6) Cash application: match the payment to the invoice and clear the receivable.

Things that go wrong: orders accepted without stock, credit limits ignored, invoices sent late, and payments that cannot be matched to an invoice. Late invoices directly delay cash.

Useful measures: order cycle time, on-time delivery rate, and days sales outstanding (DSO), which is accounts receivable divided by revenue, multiplied by the number of days. Returns and credit notes also flow through this process and must reverse both the sale and the stock movement correctly.
