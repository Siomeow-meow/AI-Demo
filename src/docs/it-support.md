# IT Support FAQ

To reset a forgotten password, use the self-service page at the company login screen and choose "Forgot password". A reset link is sent to your work email and expires after 30 minutes. If you are locked out after 5 failed attempts, contact the IT helpdesk.

Report hardware problems such as a broken laptop or monitor by raising a support ticket. Include the asset tag, a short description, and how urgent it is. High priority is for issues that stop you from working entirely.

VPN access is required when working remotely. Install the company VPN client from the software portal and sign in with your work account and the authenticator app.
