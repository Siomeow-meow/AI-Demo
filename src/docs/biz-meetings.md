# Business Lesson: Running Effective Meetings

Before a meeting, ask whether it needs to be a meeting at all; an update or a document may be enough. If it does, set a clear goal, invite only the people who are needed, share an agenda in advance and set a time limit.

During the meeting: start on time, assign a note-taker, keep to the agenda, make sure quieter people can speak, and capture decisions and action items with an owner and a date.

After the meeting, send the notes within a day with the action items. Follow up on actions at the start of the next meeting.

A useful agenda: goal and context, review of previous actions, main topics with time allowed, decisions needed, action items, and the next meeting. Recurring meetings should be reviewed regularly and cancelled if they no longer help.
