# POS Lesson: End-of-Day Closing

At closing, the cash in the drawer is reconciled against what the system says it should be.

Expected cash = opening float + cash sales - cash refunds - cash paid out. Count the drawer by denomination, then compare: the difference is over (more than expected) or short (less than expected).

Reports: an X report is a mid-day snapshot that does not reset the totals; a Z report closes the day, prints the totals and resets the counters.

Steps: run the X report, count the cash, record any variance with a reason, leave the next day's opening float, prepare the bank deposit, settle the card batch, and print the Z report. Investigate repeated variances: look at voids, refunds, wrong change and missed entries. Have a second person verify large counts, and keep deposits and the closing report together for auditing.
