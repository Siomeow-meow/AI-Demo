# General Lesson: Study Skills That Work

Active recall beats re-reading: close the notes and try to explain or answer from memory, then check. Spaced repetition means reviewing material at growing intervals (1 day, 3 days, 1 week, 1 month), which fights forgetting.

The Feynman technique: explain the topic in simple words as if teaching a beginner; wherever you get stuck, go back to the source and fill the gap.

The Pomodoro method uses 25 minutes of focused work and a 5 minute break, with a longer break after four rounds. Remove distractions such as notifications during the focus time.

Practice with real problems, not just reading. Mix related topics (interleaving) instead of repeating one type. Sleep, exercise and breaks all improve memory and focus. Set a clear goal for each study session, such as "solve five subnetting problems", rather than "study networking".
