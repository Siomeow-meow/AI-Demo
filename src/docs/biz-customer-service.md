# Business Lesson: Customer Service

A simple framework for any customer contact: listen fully, acknowledge and empathise, clarify the problem, offer a solution or clear next steps, confirm they are satisfied, and follow up.

For complaints: stay calm, do not argue or blame, apologise for the experience (even if the cause is unclear), and focus on what you can do. If you cannot solve it, say who can and by when. Do not promise things you cannot deliver.

Write clearly: short sentences, no jargon, and one clear next step. Respond within the agreed time, which is set in a service level agreement (SLA).

Measure quality with first response time, resolution time, first-contact resolution, customer satisfaction (CSAT) and Net Promoter Score (NPS). Record common questions in a knowledge base so answers stay consistent and new staff learn faster.
