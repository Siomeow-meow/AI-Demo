# General Lesson: Making the Most of On-the-Job Training

Be reliable first: arrive on time, meet deadlines, and tell your supervisor early if something will be late. Dress and communicate professionally, including emails and chat messages.

Ask good questions: try to solve it yourself for a short time, then ask with what you have tried and what you think the answer is. Write down answers so you do not need to ask twice. Take notes in meetings and confirm tasks by repeating them back.

Learn the business, not only your task: ask how your work connects to the team and customers. Offer to help with small tasks and look for work that is not covered yet. Keep a daily log of what you did and learned; it makes your final report, resume and interviews much easier.

Ask for feedback regularly, accept it without defensiveness and act on it. Respect confidentiality and company rules. Build relationships and say thank you; they may become references or job opportunities.
