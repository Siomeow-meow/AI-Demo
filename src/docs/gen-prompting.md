# General Lesson: Getting Good Answers from AI Assistants

Language models predict text; they do not look facts up unless connected to tools or documents. They can sound confident and still be wrong (hallucination), so check important facts, numbers and sources.

Better prompts: say your goal, give the context and the audience, state the format you want (a table, three bullets, an email), and give an example if you can. Break big tasks into steps. If the answer is off, tell the assistant what to change rather than starting over.

Tools and RAG make assistants more reliable: a calculator gives exact maths, and retrieval lets the assistant answer from your own documents and cite them. Ask "which document is that from?" to check the source.

Privacy: do not paste passwords, personal data or confidential material into an assistant unless your organisation has approved it. Models that run locally keep text on your own machine. Treat AI output as a draft that you remain responsible for.
