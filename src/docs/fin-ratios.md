# Finance Lesson: Financial Ratios

Liquidity: the current ratio is current assets divided by current liabilities (above 1 means short-term bills are covered); the quick ratio removes inventory first.

Leverage: debt-to-equity is total liabilities divided by equity; interest coverage is operating income divided by interest expense.

Profitability: gross margin = (revenue - cost of goods sold) / revenue; net margin = net income / revenue; return on assets (ROA) = net income / total assets; return on equity (ROE) = net income / equity.

Efficiency: inventory turnover = cost of goods sold / average inventory; days sales outstanding = receivables / revenue x days; days payable outstanding = payables / cost of goods sold x days.

Ratios only make sense when compared with the company's own history and with similar companies in the same industry. A single number rarely tells the whole story.
