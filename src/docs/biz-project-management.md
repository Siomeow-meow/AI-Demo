# Business Lesson: Project Management Basics

A project is a temporary effort to produce something specific. The triangle of scope, time and cost says that changing one affects the others; quality sits in the middle.

Plan: define the goal and what is in and out of scope, break the work into tasks (a work breakdown structure), estimate, assign owners (a RACI chart shows who is Responsible, Accountable, Consulted and Informed), and list risks with a plan for each.

Waterfall delivers in sequence (plan, build, test, release) and suits fixed requirements. Agile works in short iterations such as sprints, shows working results often and adapts to feedback.

Run it: hold short regular check-ins, track progress against milestones, report status honestly (green, amber, red), manage changes formally, and close with a lessons-learned review. A task is "done" only when it meets the agreed definition of done.
