# Business Lesson: OKRs and SWOT

SWOT is a planning snapshot: Strengths and Weaknesses are internal (your team, product, money), Opportunities and Threats are external (market, competitors, regulation, technology). Use it to connect strengths to opportunities and to prepare for threats.

OKRs (Objectives and Key Results) set goals. An Objective is a short, inspiring, qualitative goal, such as "Become the easiest supplier to work with". Key Results are 2 to 4 measurable outcomes that show progress, such as "Cut order errors from 5% to 1%". Key Results measure results, not tasks.

Tips for OKRs: set them each quarter, keep them ambitious (reaching about 70% can be a good outcome), review progress weekly, and do not tie them directly to pay. Write the activities separately as initiatives.
