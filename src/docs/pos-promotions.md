# POS Lesson: Pricing and Promotions

Markup is profit as a percentage of cost; margin is profit as a percentage of price. An item that costs 80 and sells for 100 has a 25% markup but only a 20% margin. To reach a target margin, price = cost / (1 - margin).

Discounts stack by multiplying, not adding: 20% off and then 10% off is 28% off in total, not 30%. Taking more than the margin as discount means selling at a loss, so check the margin before promoting.

Common promotions: percentage off, buy-one-get-one, bundles, volume discounts, loyalty points and time-limited sales. Psychological pricing, such as 99 instead of 100, can lift sales slightly. Always measure the result: compare sales and profit during the promotion, not just revenue, and watch for customers who only buy discounted items.

Set rules in the POS so that cashiers cannot give arbitrary discounts, and review exceptions regularly.
