# IT Lesson: Help Desk and ITIL Basics

ITIL is a popular framework for IT service management. Key terms: an incident is an unplanned interruption or reduced quality of a service (restore service fast). A problem is the underlying cause of one or more incidents (find the root cause). A service request is a standard ask, such as a new account or software. A change is a modification to the IT environment and goes through approval to reduce risk.

Support tiers: Level 1 handles first contact and common fixes; Level 2 handles deeper technical issues; Level 3 involves specialists or vendors. Escalate when you cannot solve it in the agreed time.

Priority comes from impact (how many people or how much business is affected) and urgency (how quickly it must be fixed). A priority 1 incident is critical, with a very fast response target.

Good tickets record who, what, when, the exact error message, steps already tried, and the business impact. Capture solutions in a knowledge base, and always close the loop with the user.
