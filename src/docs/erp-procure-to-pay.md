# ERP Lesson: Procure-to-Pay

Procure-to-pay (P2P) is the process of buying something and paying for it.

Steps: 1) Purchase requisition, an employee asks for something. 2) Approval by a budget owner. 3) Purchase order (PO) sent to the supplier. 4) Goods receipt, the warehouse records what actually arrived. 5) Supplier invoice received. 6) Three-way match: compare the PO, the goods receipt and the invoice. 7) Payment, ideally on the agreed terms.

Three-way match rule of thumb: never pay for more than was received, and the invoice price should match the PO price within a small tolerance. A mismatch puts the invoice on hold until it is resolved.

Key controls: approval limits by amount, segregation of duties (the person who creates a PO should not approve the payment), a vetted supplier master list, and review of duplicate invoices. Payment terms such as "2/10 net 30" give a 2% discount if paid within 10 days, otherwise the full amount is due in 30.
