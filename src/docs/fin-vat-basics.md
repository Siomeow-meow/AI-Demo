# Finance Lesson: VAT and Sales Tax Basics

VAT (value-added tax) is charged on sales and collected in stages. A VAT-registered business charges output VAT on its sales and can claim back input VAT on its purchases. It pays the government the difference.

Adding tax: price including tax = net price x (1 + rate). Removing tax from a tax-inclusive price: net = gross / (1 + rate). For example, at 12%, a gross price of 1,120 is a net of 1,000 plus 120 tax. Do not simply subtract 12% of the gross, which gives the wrong answer.

Invoices should show the net amount, the tax rate, the tax amount and the total, plus the seller's tax registration details. Keep records for the period required by local rules.

Rates, registration thresholds, exemptions and filing deadlines differ by country and change over time, so always check the current rules with the local tax authority or an accountant.
