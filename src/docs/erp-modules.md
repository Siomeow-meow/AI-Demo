# ERP Lesson: Core Modules

An ERP (Enterprise Resource Planning) system runs the main processes of a company on one shared database. Each area is a module, and because they share data, an action in one module updates the others.

Common modules: Finance (general ledger, accounts payable, accounts receivable, fixed assets), Procurement (requisitions, purchase orders, suppliers), Inventory and Warehouse (stock levels, locations, transfers), Sales and Distribution (quotes, orders, shipping, invoicing), Manufacturing (bills of materials, work orders, MRP), Human Resources and Payroll, Project Management, and Reporting or Business Intelligence.

Example of integration: when a warehouse receives goods, the stock quantity goes up, the purchase order is marked received, and a liability is recorded in accounts payable, all from one transaction.

Well-known ERP products include SAP, Oracle NetSuite, Microsoft Dynamics 365 and Odoo. Companies usually roll out modules in phases rather than all at once.
