# New Employee Onboarding

On the first day, new hires receive a laptop, an email account and badge access. HR runs an orientation covering company values, the code of conduct and the benefits enrolment process.

During the first week, each new hire meets their manager to agree on 30, 60 and 90 day goals, and is paired with a buddy from the same team to answer day-to-day questions.

Required training modules, including information security awareness and data privacy, must be completed within the first 30 days.
