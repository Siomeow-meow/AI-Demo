# Expense Reimbursement Policy

Employees may claim reasonable business expenses such as travel, client meals and software subscriptions. Claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense, with an itemised receipt attached.

Meals are reimbursed up to 800 pesos per person per day while travelling. Flights and hotels must be booked through the company travel portal. Any single expense above 10,000 pesos needs prior approval from the department head.

Reimbursements are paid in the next payroll cycle after the claim is approved by the finance team.
